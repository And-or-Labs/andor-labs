## Design Context

### Users
Adtech founders and marketing leaders assessing an experienced, hands-on GTM partner. They need credible proof, understandable engagements, visible prices, and a clear next step.

### Brand Personality
Dry, technical, playful. Plainspoken engineer's field notes, with quiet absurdity in the existing interactive figure and experiments. No em dashes, invented results, or inflated acquisition claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the approved drawing-sheet system: Newsreader, Departure Mono labels, white content, textured margins, blue rules, coral service accents, and the persistent ASCII figure. Retain the existing desktop split pane and mobile stack.

### Design Principles
- Senior operating experience precedes capabilities and delivery proof.
- Two purchases, with recurring implementation first and fixed-scope website work second.
- Reuse existing components, tokens, rules, and motion. No visual rebrand.
- Mobile, keyboard, and reduced-motion paths retain all content and actions.
- Keep proof attributable: career results, client delivery, and own-product experiments are distinct.
